Help & Glossary

⚡ Key Terms

MWp
Megawatt-peak — maximum output of a solar installation under ideal conditions.
BEE CCTS / Verra
Bureau of Energy Efficiency Carbon Credit Trading Scheme & Verra Verified Carbon Standard for registered RE offsets.
Carbon Offset
Avoided grid emissions calculated at 0.79 tCO₂e per MWh of clean generation against India's regional thermal baseline.
BESS
Battery Energy Storage System — 20 MW / 40 MWh Grid-Forming LFP VSM battery at the Hub (with ₹7.20 Cr MoP/PSDF VGF grant).
SOC
State of Charge — how full the battery is, as a percentage (0–100%).
Evening Block
The 18:00–24:00 firm power window: 60 MW peak block delivered at 100% availability using Wind + BESS + Water-Banked Hydro.
Dispatch
Scheduled release of power from a generator (hydro, battery, etc.) to the grid.
Reliability
% of the evening block hours where the 60 MW target is met. Target: 100.0% availability.
Water Bank
MWh of hydro energy conserved by day (when solar replaces hydro), available at dusk.

🌍 Why This Matters

India's power grid needs firm, schedulable renewable energy to replace coal. By combining solar (daytime), wind (nighttime), dam hydro (evening peak), and a battery buffer, SANDHYA demonstrates that a hybrid park at Gandhi Sagar can guarantee 60 MW evening peak block (18:00–24:00) of clean, dispatchable power — without building new fossil plants. The digital twin lets planners, investors, and regulators explore "what-if" scenarios before committing capital.
